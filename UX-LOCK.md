# UX lock — voice-first college/life coach

You typed the answers. The leftover forks follow the recs you signed off.

## You decided

- No invoices. No company examples. Goals are **general** (college course or a life skill).
- First act: **type a goal**, enter.
- Then **diagnostic cards** immediately. No syllabus. No “approve this plan.”
- Diagnostic = **background, then a real problem** shaped by that background.
- Background: **at most 3**, **stop early**. Mix: **sentence first**, chips only if still vague.
- Opening cap: **6 cards** (typical 1 background + 3 problems).
- After that, two paths, **auto from the goal**, **one-tap override** (College module ↔ Do it now).
  - College: teach **3 min on stage** (5–10 min later) → a few questions.
  - Life (e.g. change a tire): **how-to steps** → **confirm question** (required). “I did it” is optional and does **not** unlock a skill.
- Teach/how-to: **voice over steps on screen**. **No generated video** in MVP. If audio dies, steps still work.
- Miss: **reteach → one easier retry → move.** No infinite loop. No silent skip.
- On-screen name: **Your skills**. States: **empty / live / rust**. No points, no weapons. Six slots.
- Demo time-skip: **Skip 2 days** → refresher on a live skill → miss rusts it, hit keeps live.

## Recs you batch-accepted

- **Split view the whole time:** current card center, **Your skills** on the right from the first diagnostic card. Goal is a one-line header. Override switch next to it, small.
- Typing is the opener. Mic is **not** required. Optional later: tap a speaker on the steps.
- No chat log. If they type “explain X,” they get a card, not an essay.
- College questions after the module: **2–3**, same card UI as diagnostic problems.
- Home is a **blank goal box**, not a catalog. Three example prompts are fine as *placeholders in the box* (stats, intro CS, change a tire) — they still type or edit. Not seed courses.

## One session, on stage (~3 min after the opener)

1. Type goal.  
2. 1–3 background (stop early) → problems up to card 6. Slots start labeling, stay empty until a real check.  
3. Path guess + override.  
4. Steps + voice (3 min).  
5. 2–3 questions **or** one life confirm. Slot goes **live** or stays **empty**.  
6. Hit **Skip 2 days**. One refresher. One slot may **rust**.

Stop. That is the product.

## Out of MVP

Generated video. Invoice/company content. Chatbot. Course catalog. Honor-system unlocks. Native app. Pose-AI. More than six skills.
